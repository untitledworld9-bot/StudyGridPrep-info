What is JEE, NEET & CUET? A Complete Overview

JEE Main, NEET UG and CUET are India's most important national-level entrance examinations, conducted by NTA (National Testing Agency). Every year, over 50 lakh students compete for limited seats in India's top engineering, medical and central universities. Understanding the pattern, syllabus and strategy for each exam is essential for serious preparation.

⚛️
JEE Main 2027
Engineering entrance for IITs, NITs and GFTIs. 90 questions across Physics, Chemistry and Maths. +4/−1 marking. Conducted twice yearly by NTA.
🔬
NEET UG 2027
Medical entrance for AIIMS, JIPMER and all medical colleges. 720 marks across Biology, Physics and Chemistry. Single attempt per year by NTA.
📚
CUET UG 2027
Central Universities common entrance. Domain subjects + Language + General Test. Conducted by NTA for 250+ central universities including DU, JNU, BHU.
🎓
Board Exams 2027
CBSE Class 10 & 12 and state boards. Chapter-wise preparation, NCERT mastery and regular mock practice are key to scoring 90%+ in boards.

How to Prepare for JEE Main 2027

JEE Main preparation requires consistent daily practice across Physics, Chemistry and Maths. The most effective strategy is to first master NCERT concepts, then solve previous year questions (PYQs), and finally take full-length mock tests to build speed and accuracy. AI-powered analysis of mock tests helps identify weak topics and improve them before the actual exam.

How to Prepare for NEET UG 2027

NEET preparation is heavily NCERT-based, especially for Biology which contributes 360 out of 720 marks. Students should focus on thorough line-by-line reading of NCERT Biology, followed by Physics and Chemistry concepts. Regular mock tests and performance tracking help students understand where they stand and what to improve. In 2027, students targeting AIIMS should aim for 650+ marks with a strong Biology foundation.

How to Prepare for CUET UG 2027

CUET UG is unique because it allows students to choose domain subjects matching their desired courses. The exam tests language proficiency, domain knowledge and general aptitude. Preparation should start with understanding which universities accept which subject combinations, then mastering the NCERT syllabus for chosen domain subjects. Practice papers following the NTA pattern are essential for timing and accuracy.
